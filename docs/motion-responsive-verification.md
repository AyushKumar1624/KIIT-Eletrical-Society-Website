# Public motion and responsive behaviour

The public site retains its forest-green, ivory and gold theme, fonts and approved content. The member workspace and its styles were removed in the frontend-only cleanup.

- Short optional entrances use the existing reduced-motion support.
- Gallery photographs remain contained in their frames, with captions, keyboard navigation, Escape dismissal and opener focus restoration.
- Portrait grids adapt from phones to desktop.
- The public mobile menu isolates main/footer while open, traps keyboard focus, closes on Escape or section selection and releases its scroll lock.

Historical public checks covered 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440px with no document overflow. Gallery next/Escape and focus restoration were previously checked. These historical checks are not a substitute for physical-device testing after future design changes.

See verification.md for the checks run during the current frontend-only cleanup.
